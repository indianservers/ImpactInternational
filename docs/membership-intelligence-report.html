Delivered experience

A dedicated page under Members, with twelve URL-addressable workspaces sharing authorized filters and a premium navy, purple, white and gold visual system. Every analytical subpage has its own analysis rather than a repeated summary.

  • Overview: six KPIs, rotating world footprint, historical membership trends, category distribution, lifecycle transitions, cohort matrix, organizational rankings, renewal windows, evidence-based club scores, forecast and actionable insights.
  • Growth: D3 time-series exploration with series visibility, tooltips, zoom/pan, reset, date brushing, acquisition records and selected regional comparisons.
  • Organization: expandable hierarchy, tree and sunburst views, region/district/zone/club rankings, comparisons and configurable exploratory health weights with evidence coverage.
  • Cohorts & Retention: monthly cohort heatmap, observed retention curves, tenure distribution and category/regional retention comparisons.
  • Lifecycle: D3 Sankey of recorded from-state/to-state transition events and detailed transition counts.
  • Renewals: 7/30/60/90-day windows, validity calendar, historical renewal activity, overdue affiliations and matched conversion.
  • Categories: category, status and organizational distributions with donut, bar, treemap and packed-circle layouts; selections cross-filter other workspaces.
  • Geography: country choropleth, draggable globe, zoomable map, country/state comparisons and coordinate coverage.
  • Engagement: recorded event check-in participation, deduplicated by person/event, with timeline and club comparisons; additional event-report permission required.
  • Forecasting: actual versus projected scoped total membership, 3/6/12-month horizons, approximate prediction intervals, rolling holdout error and visible model assumptions.
  • Key Insights: deterministic acquisition anomalies, cohort changes, renewal risks, regional concentration, missing history and mandatory office vacancies.
  • Network: progressive force-directed organizational graph. Selecting a single club loads at most 50 member display names, 20 committees and 30 officer appointments. Actual committee membership edges require committee permission; officer relationships require leadership permission. No contact details are returned.

Filters include national unit, region, district, zone, club, category, status, country, state, membership type, joining and expiry ranges. Parent selections clear descendants. Filters persist in shareable URLs; sharing never grants access. CSV, Excel, labeled SVG chart exports and browser Print/Save PDF are available.

Metric definitions

  • Total members: distinct people with a qualifying scoped affiliation by the selected boundary. Affiliation totals are shown separately. People with multiple affiliations are not double-counted in summary totals; separate category and club totals need not add up.
  • Active: latest effective historical state is Active at the boundary and expiry has not passed. An absent history is Unknown, never assumed Active.
  • New: the person's earliest qualifying join within the selected organizational scope. This is distinct from gross affiliation creation and from global first-ever registration.
  • Growth: change in active people versus the opening active population, divided by that opening population. No baseline means unavailable; a small baseline can produce a very large percentage.
  • Retention: opening active people still active at the end divided by opening active people. New arrivals do not inflate the numerator.
  • Cohort retention: distinct first scoped joins grouped by month, evaluated at the displayed month boundary. Future observations and incomplete historical coverage are null, not zero or churn.
  • Tenure: elapsed time from first scoped join to the selected end, averaged across people. Display rounding can show zero for very recent populations.
  • Renewals: actual renewal events are separate from distinct eligible expiring affiliations. Conversion matches distinct eligible affiliations to recorded renewal outcomes. Upcoming windows are cumulative. For a past period, upcoming begins on the following local day.
  • Lifecycle: recorded transitions, not a mutually exclusive person funnel. One person can contribute multiple events.
  • Engagement: actual nonvoid event check-ins within the authorized scope and period. Missing attendance evidence is not fabricated engagement.
  • Officer coverage: filled active mandatory positions divided by mandatory positions, based on recorded appointments at the selected boundary.
  • Club health: weighted mean of available growth, retention, engagement, officer and renewal dimensions. Missing dimensions are excluded; coverage reports their available weight share. Weights apply to the current analytical session.

Boundaries follow the tenant time zone. Current periods end at the current instant; past dates include the selected local day. Date buckets can be daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly.

Backend and scope

GET /api/v1/membership/analytics returns aggregated datasets selected by workspace. GET /api/v1/membership/analytics/export?format=csv|xlsx exports authorized aggregates with audit logging and formula-safe text. Both use the same filter engine.

The service reuses Persons, Memberships, MembershipHistory, MembershipRenewals, organizational units, categories, applications, appointments, committees and event/check-in data. No analytics tables, migrations or duplicate member workflows were introduced.

Authentication, members.view, tenant and organizational scope are enforced server-side; members.export additionally gates downloads. Leadership, committees and event datasets have additional permission checks. Typed SQL parameters protect filter values. Ordinary members cannot access the analytical endpoint; unauthorized hierarchy selections are rejected. The 20-second aggregate cache rechecks authorization before reuse, includes scope and grants in its key, has a 64 MB size bound, and supports manual refresh bypass.

Outstanding data and interpretation limits

Historical evidence is preserved as observed. No historical membership or prediction values were invented to resemble the mockup.
  • The live seeded population has little long-term history. A large recent import can dominate growth; historical QA fixtures exist only in the separate test database.
  • Projection models future first-join acquisitions added to total people. It does not invent future active status, churn, renewals, transfers or club openings. Six completed monthly observations with recorded population are required. Intervals are approximate, not guarantees.
  • No clubs in the tested seed have recorded coordinates. Country shading is available; coordinate pins remain absent. State boundaries are not installed, so states use comparison charts rather than fabricated map detail.
  • Current membership/category/geography associations define the selected scope. Full historical organizational reassignment, historical committee rosters or deleted appointment history cannot be reconstructed unless recorded in the source system.
  • Member record drill-downs preserve filters but display the current directory status, with a visible notice for historical analytical dates. They are not an invented historical profile snapshot.
  • SVG exports cover SVG visualizations with labels/legends. The semantic HTML cohort matrix is included in analytical data exports and print/PDF, not converted into an SVG chart.
  • Health scores are exploratory and explicitly show evidence coverage. They should not be read as established performance targets.
  • Aggregate cache results can be up to 20 seconds old; use Refresh to bypass. Cold large-population requests remain slower than cached views.
