# Phase 5 completion report — unified communications

Delivery date: 11 November 2026. Phase 5 application code, additive migrations, APIs, persistent screens, workers and local verification are implemented. Live Meta/Twilio/SMTP provider activation and production deployment have **not** been verified. No real member received test traffic. The limitations below are part of this delivery, rather than claims that every aspirational brief item is complete.

## Implemented behavior

The Communication Center extends existing identity, people/membership, arbitrary organizational hierarchy, effective scoped RBAC, shared independent governance approvals, audit and PostgreSQL mail outbox. It does not create a second member store, approval engine or bulk sender. Access keys are communications.view, send, manage, inbox, reports and configure. Current workspace, organizational subtree, committee/event participation and explicit conversation participation govern access. A senior title alone cannot read private conversations.

Private, group, support, committee and event conversations persist messages, replies, internal staff notes, read/unread state, mute, assignments, priority and tags. Authorized staff can claim an unassigned inbound support inquiry before accessing its history. Members do not receive staff notes or private CRM context. Keyset inbox/history APIs and dynamic TanStack virtualization bound response/DOM work. WebSockets send small invalidations with precise update markers and recheck current session/workspace/roles/participants once per second; reconnects reload authorized resources. Delivery transitions also invalidate affected conversations.

Private PDF, PNG and JPEG files use protected authenticated downloads and random-key private filesystem storage. Signature/extension, size and count checks apply. External draft messages hold dispatch until uploads finish. WhatsApp supports text and a single image/PDF in supported contexts; signed inbound media metadata creates a durable download job with trusted-host checks, disabled redirects, streaming bounds and retries.

Announcements support independently reviewed publication, audience visibility, pinning, expiry, read and acknowledgement records. Campaigns use a seven-step wizard, typed bounded nested AND/OR segmentation, hierarchy and consent filters, a limited preview, frozen deduplicated recipients, independent approvals and frozen personalization context. Submitted content, immutable revisions and recipient identity are database guarded. Reviewers see actual content and a private audience preview. Later event/organization edits cannot silently change approved personalized content. Approved replacement content requires a new reviewed version. Cancellation suppresses unsent work.

Saved audiences, templates and version history, scheduled/recurring and event-relative sends, and independently approved automations are persisted. Supported triggers include birthday/anniversary, renewal/officer-term thresholds and supported event notification sources. Birthday messages require privacy permission and occasion opt-in. Source-linked executions are unique; notification provenance includes EventId. Editing a rule invalidates approval. Template versions are pinned. Source notifications produced before activation cannot trigger a new rule retroactively.

Send-time checks include current sender authority, verified contact route, channel/purpose consent, suppressions, recipient quiet hours, provider window/template capability and database-backed quotas. DST gap/ambiguity and half-hour cases are checked. Promotional opt-out does not disable operational messages. Legacy privacy choices remain respected.

Existing outbox jobs receive bounded asynchronous fan-out, security-mail priority, PostgreSQL coordination, retry/backoff and dead-letter handling. Communication dispatch commits a claim before network I/O. Interrupted/ambiguous submissions become Uncertain for reconciliation instead of an automatic duplicate send. SMTP acceptance is distinct from delivery. Legacy authentication mail retains at-least-once behavior; universal exactly-once delivery is not claimed.

Actual status/channel/trend, attempted/pending, campaign and announcement acknowledgement analytics use persisted records. Unknown legacy creation dates are excluded from date-period statistics. Unavailable opens/clicks/costs are labeled unavailable. Configurable message/object retention redacts bounded batches while preserving record identity and delivery provenance. AI is explicitly disabled and private conversation content is not transmitted to an AI provider.

## PostgreSQL migrations and storage

Five additive migrations bring the total to **26**, all applied successfully to a fresh empty PostgreSQL database and the isolated demo/test/scale/transport databases:

- `20261111000507_CommunicationFoundation`: domain tables, outbox extensions, scoped permissions, constraints, indexes and immutable approval/recipient guards.
- `20261111002847_CommunicationProviderBinding`: provider account binding and frozen template version.
- `20261111003141_CommunicationOutboxDefaults`: compatible defaults and legacy email outbox backfill.
- `20261111005121_CommunicationInboundMedia`: durable private inbound media jobs.
- `20261111005801_CommunicationProvenanceAndLimits`: event provenance and sender/report query indexes.

Sixteen new tables: CommunicationAccounts, Conversations, ConversationParticipants, CommunicationMessages, CommunicationAttachments, CommunicationPreferences, CommunicationSuppressions, CommunicationTemplates, CommunicationRevisions, CommunicationAudiences, CommunicationBroadcasts, CommunicationRecipients, CommunicationProviderEvents, CommunicationAutomations, CommunicationExecutions and CommunicationPolicies. Fifteen are tenant-qualified; preferences belong to the existing user. Existing Mails is extended rather than duplicated. Files reside outside the database; metadata and authorization remain in PostgreSQL. See [schema](docs/schema.sql).

Apply migrations before serving traffic. Compose now persists private objects and Data Protection keys separately; the image creates writable directories for its unprivileged account. nginx forwards WebSocket upgrades and preserves request authority. Outer HTTPS proxy forwarding, Docker image builds, Compose startup and shared-volume operation have not been exercised on this machine.

## Provider integration status and delivery evidence

Meta and Twilio adapters make real HTTP requests through HttpClient; SMTP uses the existing transport. Production endpoints are fixed official hosts. Mock endpoints are restricted to Development. Credentials are Data Protection encrypted and excluded from response serialization; callback verification tokens are hashed. Provider status and capability checks are explicit.

Meta sender identity is checked by phone lookup; deployment must select a supported Graph version. Supported template sync reads the first 100 templates and enables only approved simple BODY-only templates with zero or one positional member-name parameter. Unsupported components/variables remain inactive. Service-window checks, approved-template dispatch, signed inbound text/media and multiple phone-number routing are implemented. Twilio verifies sender ownership and uses its configured exact callback URL in signature validation. SMTP communication sender activation requires exact configured From plus a deployment operator allowlist after actual provider/domain verification. This allowlist is an operator assertion, not automated DNS certification.

**37 protocol checks passed against local HTTP and SMTP sinks**, using the actual adapters, API, workers and PostgreSQL. Coverage includes approved parameterized Meta sends; signed delivery/read events; signature failure, deduplication and out-of-order handling; old timestamps not reopening the service window; multi-number routing; inbound claim/privacy; private PDF send/receive and blocked untrusted media host; 100 concurrent signed callbacks; SMS transient retry and permanent failure; interrupted dispatch quarantine; actual SMTP MIME/encoded HTML, attachments, acceptance semantics; signed unsubscribe confirmation/one-click; bounce suppression. SMTP inbound correctly reports unavailable. Sink accounts are disabled and child processes stopped after testing.

No real Meta/Twilio credential, public callback, SMTP TLS/domain, external recipient, carrier registration, provider quota, billable cost or deliverability was verified. The email HMAC callback requires a trusted deployment adapter and is not a native SES/SNS integration. Provider docs informing the protocol include [Meta message example](https://www.postman.com/meta/whatsapp-business-platform/request/8gvd47s/send-text-message), [Meta webhook reference](https://whatsapp.github.io/WhatsApp-Nodejs-SDK/api-reference/webhooks/start/), [Twilio request security](https://www.twilio.com/docs/usage/security) and [Twilio message resource](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/api/message-resource). These references are not evidence of live account acceptance.

## Measured local performance

See [scale measurements](docs/phase5-scale.json), [query plans](docs/phase5-explain.json) and [signed callback load](docs/phase5-webhook-load.json). PostgreSQL 17.6 and API ran on this shared local Windows machine over loopback. The isolated fixture contained 101,035 people, 10,030 organizations, 10,002 conversations, 200,008 messages, 200,000 frozen recipients and 50,037 users after accumulated runs.

Two concurrent 50,000-recipient snapshots completed in **21.70 seconds each**. This is bounded database snapshot work, not synchronous external delivery. The durable worker was interrupted with 750 outbox rows; all 750 remained after restart. Both campaigns resumed to **100,000 recipient jobs**, with **zero duplicate messages**. Approval state was explicitly installed by benchmark SQL only, to isolate fan-out; correctness suites separately exercise real independent review.

Ten fictional authenticated users issued 100 concurrent inbox requests during processing: p50 82.36ms, **p95 148.31ms**, p99 164.20ms, zero errors. Ten initial post-restart inbox reads measured p95 101.95ms. Fifty warm sequential samples measured p95 inbox **60.47ms**, history **42.65ms**, analytics **60.85ms**, zero errors. The historical initial checkpoint in `phase5-scale-initial.json` used a different fixture/cache state and is not a controlled before/after optimization comparison.

The separate 100-callback/10-worker signed webhook run records its latest p50/p95/p99 in the linked JSON; every request succeeded. It exercises local signature/transaction/dedup paths, not public network/provider capacity. No production latency SLO, sustained external bulk throughput or thousands-of-WebSockets capacity is asserted. Redis was not added without measurement demonstrating a need.

## Known limitations and operational follow-up

- Activate real providers with verified domains/numbers, correct public HTTPS callbacks, maintained API versions, registered sender/country policies and controlled consenting recipients before claiming live integration. Inbound SMTP is unsupported. Unknown nonmember inbound inquiries can be claimed but outbound replies require linked verified consenting membership; no identity is silently created.
- Meta templates support the stated simple subset and first-page sync, rather than every component/language variant. Message bodies render safe encoded text with limited bold formatting, not arbitrary HTML/WYSIWYG. Library lookups are capped; not every library screen offers unbounded paging.
- Analytics do not yet provide full response/resolution timing, advanced campaign/event/template drilldown or currency-aware cost aggregation. Opens/clicks and unreported costs stay unavailable; no tracking pixels are fabricated.
- WebSockets use one-second database polling and reconnect reload. Multi-replica/high-connection load, public TLS proxy operation, backups/disaster recovery and full accessibility review remain to be validated. File storage requires private persistent shared storage for replicas; no S3 adapter, malware scanner, EXIF cleaning or complete orphan recovery is supplied.
- Recurrence/event offsets and approved source-linked automations are implemented; arbitrary visual sequences/cron, every conceivable trigger, and chained broadcast automation are not. Occasion date selection uses the implemented calendar policy; recipient quiet hours use the recipient timezone.
- AI is disabled, with no live assistant adapter. Existing Phase 1 privileged identity hardening and earlier-phase deferred items remain as documented in their reports. Paid booking, elections and finance are not introduced here.

## Phase 6 dependencies

Projects and meetings should reuse existing people, scoped organizations, committee responsibilities, event/session participation, shared approvals, conversations, frozen audiences/context, pinned templates, consent/suppressions, source-linked execution IDs and the existing outbox. New sources must provide durable provenance and idempotency and must not create another member, notification, approval or scheduling engine. Define project/meeting-specific privacy and effective responsibility before enabling communications. Provider deployment verification remains a separate prerequisite for real external delivery.
